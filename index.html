Our Leadership

Rev. Peter Kamau

Rev. Peter Kamau, a dedicated shepherd of faith, serves as the pastor of Kingdomcity Church. With a heart aflame with passion for God and a commitment to the spiritual well-being of the congregation, Rev. peter embodies the essence of servant leadership.

His insightful teachings and compassionate pastoral care have become guiding lights, illuminating the path of Kingdomcity Church on its spiritual journey. His leadership extends beyond the pulpit, reaching into the hearts of individuals, inspiring transformative growth, and nurturing a sense of unity among the diverse members of our church.

True leadership begins with serving others as Christ served us.
